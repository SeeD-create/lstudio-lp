About this concept

AKARI Journeys is a fictional brand created for a website proposal. It is not connected to a real travel provider. There are no bookable trips, prices or customer recommendations.

All photographs are AI-generated illustrations and do not represent confirmed destinations or accommodation. German and English copy is draft content without independent native-speaker review.

The form is processed only in your browser’s working memory. The application does not send enquiries, collect contact details, set cookies, run analytics or save entries. The hosting provider may generate technical access logs when the website loads.

WordPress, email delivery and news management are not connected in this static concept. Real operator information and the privacy notice must be prepared and checked against the actual service before launch.
